## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 🧪 MPS-QCBM vs SMOTE — Privacy-Aware Synthetic Patients
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/STATUS-RESEARCH-9DB8F0?style=flat-square) ![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/REPO-PRIVATE-555?style=flat-square)

A matrix product state (MPS) Born machine, simulated classically, that generates synthetic diabetic patients to rebalance a 96,146-patient prediction dataset. It is benchmarked against SMOTE under preregistered protocols.

- **Result:** matches SMOTE's classification accuracy, with significantly lower measured disclosure risk. A membership-inference attack scored **AUC 0.508 vs 0.561** against SMOTE (p = 4.3 × 10⁻⁷, 10 of 10 splits, replicated on unseen splits).
- **Rigour:** frozen protocols, Holm-corrected paired tests, noise and copula baselines, SHAP analysis, and an independent recomputation of every headline number.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch, tensor networks (TT-SVD), XGBoost, imbalanced-learn, SHAP

**Links:** 🔒 Private repository · available on request
